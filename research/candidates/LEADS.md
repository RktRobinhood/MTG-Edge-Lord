# Leads

Links the daily finds run turned up on sources `docs/SOURCES.md` excludes from automated access — Reddit above all. The run does not open them.

The route that is open is the one `docs/SOURCES.md` describes: a person reads the public thread, writes a short factual summary in their own words, and files it through `research/inbox/`. `research/inbox/2026-09-20-massimo-vraska.json` is the worked example.

Newest first. Delete a lead once it has been read, filed or dismissed.
